# Marketing videos

For work on the promo and stories videos. The general rules are in `AGENTS.md`.

- Separate projects this repo works with (the video project) are found through the git-ignored `external-projects.json`, one section each with its `folder`, `readme` and `rules`; copy `external-projects.example.json` if it's missing, and ask where a folder is if its path doesn't exist. Never write their paths into docs or code; name the section instead, and add a section to both files for a new project.
- The promo and stories videos are made in a separate repo, the video project (`video_marketing` in `external-projects.json`). Read its `readme` and `rules` before working there, and follow them: they cover paid voice and music credits and capturing real screens.
- After a release people can see in the videos (screens, the logo), offer to recapture and re-render them; their screens come from this app on the emulators.
