# Sharing and encryption

Conventions for shared items (notes, tasks, finances, health logs) and end-to-end encryption. Read before changing how items are stored, shared or unlocked. The general rules are in `AGENTS.md`.

- Notes, tasks, finances and health logs are shared through `permissions.read` and `permissions.write` lists of user ids; `owner` is the creator. Keep queries, writes and `firestore.rules` consistent with each other: an update must match one of the cases in `canUpdateShared` (owner, a writer changing the content fields, anyone pinning or archiving for themselves, a reader leaving, someone invited accepting or declining). Sharing invites: the person goes in `invites.read` / `invites.write` and joins `permissions` only by accepting; the owner can't add anyone to `permissions` directly, and a new item starts with only its creator. The app invites one person per update, appended to `invites.read`, so the rules can refuse someone who blocked the owner (`users/{uid}/blocked/{id}`, private to its owner). A new content field goes in that collection's list in the rules and in `contentFields` (`app/lib/utils/encrypted-document.ts`), so it is encrypted too.
- Shared items are read and written through `app/apis/firestore/shared-document.ts` (create, update, share, leave, subscribe), which handles end-to-end encrypted items and plain ones alike; a collection module only adds what is specific to it (pinning, deleting). Never write an item's content fields directly, or an encrypted item would get readable text.
- Only content edits set `updatedAt`. Pinning, archiving, sharing and leaving must not, or they change the "edited" date and the list order.
